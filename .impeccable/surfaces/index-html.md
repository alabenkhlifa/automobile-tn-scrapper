## Scope

One dashboard app at `index.html` with four routes: Neuf TN, Occasion TN (9annas), Europe, Historique. Replaces `used_cars_dashboard.html`, `autoscout24_dashboard.html`, `new_cars_history.html` (kept as redirects). Visitor mode: Operate.

## Audience and job

Portfolio visitors confirm the pipeline is real and live; market watchers check what moved. Bilingual FR/EN. Freshness per source is always visible; automobile.tn is frozen since 2026-05-04 and says so.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard, played straight at Stripe Dashboard's craft level: a calm analytics app where every number names its source and date. Refuses themed metaphors and the card-grid-of-charts pile.
OWN-WORLD: White ground, slate ink #0F172A, muted #64748B, hairline #E2E8F0, one indigo accent #4F46E5 for data and selection, green/red only for price direction, amber only for stale data. Inter, tabular figures. 1px borders, 10px radius, no shadows beyond a hairline.
STORY: Visitors see the market's median, its change and volume, drill into breakdowns, and search real listings or trims, then switch market from the sidebar.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Sidebar (232px) with four dashboards and source freshness dots; header with title, source line, date range and export; four KPI tiles with deltas; main time-series chart left, breakdown card right; table starts below the fold line.
FORM: Canon (standing exit) against Stripe Dashboard; seed b957f848, safer register.
